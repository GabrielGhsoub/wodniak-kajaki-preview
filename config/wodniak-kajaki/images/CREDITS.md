## What is VERIFIED, from his own route and price pages
- Pricing is PER BOAT PER DAY and already includes the shuttle: "Ceny obejmuja
  koszty transportu".
- 2026 promo rate for a two person kayak: 120 zl on Krutyn to Ukta, Krutyn to
  Rosocha and Krutyn to the mill; 150 zl on Krutyn to Nowy Most. The promo runs
  all of 2026, weekends included, so it is what a customer actually pays.
- Child add on seat ("dostawka") is 10 zl above the two person rate.
- Distances and durations are quoted from his pages: 15 km 3 to 4 h, 9 km 2 to
  3 h, 5 km about 1 h, 22 km about 6 h.
- Extras at his published prices: tent 70, stove 30, dry barrel 10, dry bag 10,
  bike for a day 50. Buoyancy aids are free ("Kamizelki asekuracyjne
  bezplatnie").
- He publishes NO departure times: "Nie ograniczamy czasu trwania splywow", and
  pickup is by phone. So this books a DATE and a BOAT, never a time slot. That
  is why kind is "experience" and not "session".

## What is ABSENT, and therefore not in the demo
- No published season, no opening hours, so no blocked dates are invented.
- No deposit amount anywhere, though he does require a "zaliczka". Hence
  bookingMode "request": an estimate, nothing charged, he confirms.
- No prices at all for weekend, multi day, school or corporate trips. Those are
  individually quoted and are deliberately NOT modelled as bookable units.
- He offers boats from 1 to 5 seats plus canoes, each at its own published
  price. The demo shows the two person rate only, because the engine has no
  single select option group. Worth saying plainly if he asks.

Private demonstration only. Not part of the public showcase.
